04 Projects

Aug 2026 Present Individual · In Progress

Towards Automating Linkography

A research project building an NLP pipeline to automate linkography the design-protocol analysis method that segments designer transcripts into "design moves" and traces the associative links between them. Drafted rule-based criteria (speaker change, clause splitting, subject/object/action tracking via spaCy dependency parsing and cosine similarity) to automatically detect design-move boundaries, then automated link formation between moves using sentence-embedding similarity, following and extending the Fuzzy Linkography methodology. Validated the pipeline against manually coded ground-truth transcripts, reaching ~95% move-segmentation accuracy and comparing one-one and weighted link correspondence across 20+ transcripts.

Python spaCy NLP Sentence Transformers Design Research
